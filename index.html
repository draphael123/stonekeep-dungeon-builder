ARCHITECT'S GUIDE

Shape the darkness.

Build

Drag the left mouse button over empty tiles. Adjacent chambers automatically receive connecting doorways.

Inspect

Click a room to select it. Press R to rotate its footprint, or Delete to remove it.

Decorate

Place a decoration, return to the Stone Room tool, then click it to rotate, resize, vary, duplicate, or remove it.

Dress rooms

Select a room, choose its condition, then auto-dress it. The throne-hall preset creates a cinematic hero chamber.

Build across layers

Use Surface for halls, homes, roads, farms, and workshops. Switch Underground to excavate and manage the dungeon beneath the same site.

Navigate

Right-drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, and use WASD or arrows to pan the build camera.

Explore

Enter Explore, click the view, then use WASD and the mouse. Escape releases the pointer.
